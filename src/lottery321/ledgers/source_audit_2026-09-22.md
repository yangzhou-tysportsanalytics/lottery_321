# Source audit of the draft-rights ledgers (2026-09-22)

This file is the public record of the two audit rounds described in the paper's Online Appendix E.2. Both rounds are dated 2026-09-22, the day of the run registration. The ledgers and routers in `src/lottery321/ledgers/` are the versions the runs read; no router was changed in round 2.

**Scope (67 items).** (1) Every native pick coded with medium or low confidence and every pool or swap encoded in the routers; (2) every assumption written into a router; (3) ten high-confidence protected (or reverse-protected) picks drawn at random (`random.seed(20260922); random.sample(L, 10)` over the 58 high-confidence `protected`/`reverse` entries in year and team order). Rows marked "extra" were checked along the way and are counted.

**Sources.** The ledgers were compiled from Hoops Rumors deadline lists and realized holders from NBA.com. Round 1 checked each item against sources other than Hoops Rumors: trade reports and explainers from the time of each trade (NBA.com, CBS, SI, Yahoo and local outlets), and ProSportsTransactions (PST) and RealGM transaction text. Round 2 read the PST draft-year pages, future-pick pages and transaction records that round 1 could not reach, from copies saved by the authors on 2026-09-22 (the saved pages are not redistributed), plus RealGM and news sources.

**Verdicts.** CONFIRMED: an independent source states the encoded term, or a term equivalent to it at every slot. CONFLICT: an independent source states a different term. UNVERIFIABLE: no source other than Hoops Rumors found stating it. ASSUMPTION: an encoded choice rule or order that no source states; it stays a documented assumption.

## Summary

| | CONFIRMED | CONFLICT | UNVERIFIABLE / ASSUMPTION | items |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Round 1 (ledger version 0.1) | 56 | 2 | 9 | 67 |
| After round 2 (version 0.2) | 63 | 0 | 4 | 67 |

Round 1 found two conflicts. The 2021 Lakers-to-New Orleans boundary (sources said "top 8"; the router conveys at 1-7) was closed in round 2 by the PST wording "protected #8-30 in 2021", which confirms the router. For the 2026 Oklahoma City/Clippers/Houston pool, round 1 led to one correction of the 2026 router (when Houston keeps its pick, Philadelphia receives the better and Dallas the worse of the Oklahoma City and Clippers picks); round 2 confirmed the corrected encoding from PST. Of the nine items round 1 could not verify, round 2 confirmed five; the other four remain assumptions: two definitional choice rules (2021 Houston, 2025 Oklahoma City) and two with consistent but not explicit support (the 2024 Washington branch at slots 13-30 and the order of Utah's 2026 swaps). The branch probabilities of all four are checked in `results/ledger_assumption_check.json`.
